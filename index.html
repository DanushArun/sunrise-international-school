Our Heritage

65 Years of Shaping Futures

Founded in 1960 by the visionary S.K.Velayutham, our school has been a beacon of education in Kurinjipadi for over six decades. As a government-aided institution, we provide quality education accessible to all.

Rooted in the rich cultural heritage of Kurinjipadi - a town famous for its handloom traditions - we instill values of hard work, dedication, and excellence in every student who walks through our gates.
