## Key Changes

### Visual Design
- **Clean, Professional Layout** - Inspired by ASB India's minimalist approach
- **Modern Color Scheme** - Navy blue (#003366), secondary blue (#0066cc), accent orange (#ff6b35)
- **Premium Typography** - Montserrat for headings, Open Sans for body text
- **Generous White Space** - Clean, breathable layout
- **Subtle Animations** - Professional scroll-triggered effects
- **Modern Cards** - Clean borders, hover effects, shadows

### Branding
- **Updated to SKV School** - Customized for your actual school
- **Professional Logo** - Bold, letter-spaced branding
- **Tagline Added** - "Inspiring Excellence Since 1998"
